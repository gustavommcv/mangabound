# Code of Conduct

Mangabound welcomes participants of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels. These rules apply to issues, pull requests, other project spaces, and anyone officially representing the project.

## Participation

- Treat others respectfully and consider different viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep feedback constructive and focused on the work, not the person. Disagreement is welcome.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy. Ask permission before sharing someone else's personal information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, credit others' contributions, and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwelcome sexual attention, inappropriate sexualized content, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are impersonation, evading participation restrictions, spam, or promoting these behaviors.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation privately to [monnerat.gustavo@outlook.com](mailto:monnerat.gustavo@outlook.com). Reports are received by the repository owner, [@gustavommcv](https://github.com/gustavommcv). Include relevant links and context, sharing only the personal information needed to assess the incident. Do not post incident reports in public issues or pull requests.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, use the separate private reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer response

The maintainer reviews reports fairly, considering their context, severity, and any repeated behavior. Reports are handled privately, with personal information shared only as needed to assess or address the incident. This is a volunteer-maintained project; no fixed response time is promised.

Depending on the situation, the maintainer may request a change in behavior, remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Serious violations may lead to immediate restrictions. Decisions will be explained to those affected where appropriate, while respecting privacy.
