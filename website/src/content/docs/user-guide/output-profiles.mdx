## Device and format

Choose your model under **Device**. To set the page size yourself, open **Advanced conversion options** and fill in **Custom width** and **Custom height** in pixels. A custom device profile requires both dimensions.

| Format | When to choose it                                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| EPUB   | A book with chapter navigation; the single-series-book mode requires it        |
| CBZ    | Processed images in a ZIP archive; often a straightforward choice for KOReader |
| PDF    | Fixed pages for a reader or tablet that supports PDF                           |

Compatibility depends on the reader application as well as the device. Kindle's native reader does not directly open EPUB: use a compatible transfer/conversion service or an installed reader such as KOReader. KOReader treats EPUB differently from CBZ/PDF; see [reader settings](../../koreader/recommended-settings/).

## Advanced conversion options

**Advanced conversion options** exposes mangapress's settings, grouped by purpose. Start with the device defaults; change one setting at a time and check the resulting book.

- **Reading** says what the images are. **Content** is **Manga or comic pages**, where each image is a page, or **Webtoon strips**, where each chapter is joined into one long strip and cut into pages between panels. **Manga reading order** reads right to left and puts the right half of a split spread first.
- **Double-page spreads** decides what becomes of a page that is two pages wide. **Wide pages** splits it in two, keeps it whole as one page, or does both. When the whole spread is kept you can put it before its halves (**Whole spread first**), leave it upright instead of turned on its side (**Keep the whole spread upright**, to read with the device in landscape), or turn it the other way (**Rotate clockwise**). **Restack 4-panel strips as 2×2** is for pages made of two tall strips, and replaces the handling of wide pages.
- **Size and borders**: **Page size** fits each page to the screen, with or without enlarging small ones, stretches it, or crops it to fill the screen. **Borders** are automatic (each page's own background), white, or black.
- **Cropping** removes margins, with optional page-number detection, and the empty space between panels. If artwork is cut off, reduce or disable cropping.
- **Two-page view** is for readers that show two pages side by side, such as Kobo's and Kindle's own: which side the book starts on, a single centered page, and the direction pages turn. It applies to EPUB only, and **KOReader ignores these**.
- **Color and tone**: **Color pages** converts every page to grayscale or keeps in color the pages that have real color; it follows the device, so choosing a reader with a color screen turns it on and choosing any other turns it off. **Autocontrast** stretches tones to full black and white for black-and-white pages only, for color pages too, or not at all. **Reduce rainbow effect** reduces certain moiré artifacts on color e-ink screens; compare results on your own pages. An empty **Gamma** uses the device's own, not zero.
- **Page images**: **Page format** saves each page as a JPEG with every tone, or as a PNG reduced to the grays the screen can show. For PNG pages, **Keep all 256 grays** skips that reduction, **8-bit PNG** is for readers that cannot open the more compact kind, and **Color pages as PNG too** also saves as PNG the pages kept in color, which otherwise stay JPEG. All three make larger files. An empty **JPEG quality** uses the device default; the field says what it still applies to, since an EPUB's cover is a JPEG whatever the pages are.

**Use the images as they are**, beside the device, packages the pages untouched: nothing is cropped, resized, converted to grayscale or saved again. Use it for images that were already prepared for your reader. Everything that would change an image is locked while it is on. The reading order, the webtoon cut and the two-page view still apply, and an EPUB's cover is still made from the first image.

**Cover** decides how each book's cover is made from its first image. **Cut the front cover from a wide image** is for a first image that is a whole jacket or a spread: the cover becomes its front only, on the side the reading order puts it. **Crop the cover to fill the screen** crops the cover, and enlarges a small one, until it is exactly the size of the screen; without it the cover is only shrunk to fit. An EPUB always has this cover, a CBZ gets one only when the front is cut from a wide image, and a PDF has none.

For a Kobo, an EPUB is named `.kepub.epub`, the name Kobo's own reader looks for. **Name the book .epub**, beside the device, gives it the plain name instead; nothing inside the book changes. It applies only to a Kobo's EPUB at the device's own size.

An option that another choice leaves without effect stays visible, dimmed, and says why. With **Webtoon strips**, for example, reading order, wide pages, borders and margin cropping are locked, because webtoon strips never use them; what you had chosen comes back when you return to pages.

Modified fields can be reset individually, or together with **Reset to defaults**. Settings are kept between launches.

## One book for the series

Enable **Create one book for the series** in the queue to combine its chapters and volumes into one book, with chapters nested under their volumes in the table of contents.

This mode uses EPUB and keeps both process steps enabled. Turn it off to change the format or steps. Larger combined books can take longer to open on an e-reader.
