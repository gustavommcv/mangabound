## From chapters to books

Mangabound brings volume organization, conversion, and local sharing into one desktop app.

Choose a chapter folder, a library of manga folders, or a CBZ. Check the volumes, set your device and format, and process the queue. You can then save the books or share them with your reader.

## Behind the scenes

Two tools handle the processing:

- **[mangabind](https://github.com/gustavommcv/mangabind)** groups chapters into volumes.
- **[mangapress](https://github.com/gustavommcv/mangapress)** adjusts the pages and creates EPUB, CBZ, or PDF books.

Both come with Mangabound.

## Local processing

Organization, conversion, and saving happen on your computer. Online searches for volume information or an author are optional. They use the title you enter and the selected work's identifier and language where needed; your manga pages stay local.

Network sharing is enabled from the **Share** panel. Keep the app open while your reader downloads the books.

## Known limitations

Mangabound is an alpha.

- Combining a whole series into one book supports EPUB only, not CBZ or PDF.
- The author is written into EPUB and PDF books only. A CBZ carries no author.
- Pending books are local to this installation and are not a backup. Once a conversion has been deleted, or cleaned up after all its books were saved, getting its books again means processing the source again.
- Sharing the ready books shares all of them, including old conversions that were never saved or deleted.
- A link in a manga folder, or in a shared folder, that leads outside that folder is not followed.
- The app is in English only.
- There are no automatic updates: download and install each release yourself.
- The installers are not code-signed or notarized, and no build attestation is published.
- macOS builds are for Apple Silicon only. There is no AppImage, and the Arch package is a direct download, not an AUR submission.
