## 1. Add your manga

Drag a manga folder or CBZ into the queue, or use **Files** or **Folder**. Clicking the drop area lets you choose what to add.

A folder holds separate chapters. A single CBZ is treated as one complete volume; for separate chapter CBZs, add their containing folder instead.

## 2. Check the volumes

For a chapter folder, use the **Edit volumes** action to check its mapping.

- **File names** summarizes volumes already recognized from chapter names.
- **Manual** lets you create volumes and assign selected chapters or a chapter range.
- **Online source** optionally searches MangaDex for a suggestion. Check its result before confirming.

If no volumes were recognized, the editor opens **Manual** with an explanation. You can organize everything offline; an online entry is not required. See [volume mapping](../../user-guide/mapping-editor/) for the steps.

## 3. Choose your device and format

In **Steps**, keep **Group chapters into volumes** and **Convert for e-reader** enabled to do both. Select **Device** and **Format**. Use **Advanced conversion options** only if you need to change image settings.

Want the entire series in one book? Enable **Create one book for the series**. This mode uses EPUB and keeps both steps enabled. Turn it off to change the format or steps.

## 4. Process

Click **Process** (or **Process N items** for a queue). The app shows overall progress; **Show details** reveals individual volume progress where available. Larger books take longer, and saving can continue after page processing finishes.

## 5. Save or share

Use **Save as…** beside a book, or **Save all to folder…** for several books. You can save again to a different destination without converting again while the pending books remain available.

**Share these books** lets KOReader download them over your local network, even before you export a copy. Follow the [sharing guide](../../koreader/opds-sharing/).

<TutorialImage
  name="generated/save-results"

/>

:::note[Do not discard your only copy]
Unsaved finished books remain under **Ready books** after restarting the app. **Delete** removes a conversion's pending books, not just its row. Save anything you want to keep first. [How saving and deletion work](../../user-guide/exporting/).
:::
