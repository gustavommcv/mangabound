Once you have started sharing in Mangabound, connecting KOReader takes less than a minute.

---

## Step-by-Step Connection

<Steps>
1. ### Verify Wi-Fi Network
   Make sure your e-reader (Kindle, Kobo, or Android device) is connected to the **same local Wi-Fi network** as the computer running Mangabound.

2. ### Open OPDS in KOReader
   From the main KOReader File Manager:
   * Tap the **Search icon (magnifying glass)** in the top menu bar.
   * Select **OPDS catalog**.

   :::tip[Media Demonstration Placeholder]
   > 📸 **Screenshot**: KOReader top navigation showing the search menu with "OPDS catalog" highlighted.
   :::

3. ### Add the Mangabound Catalog
   * Tap **Add new catalog** (or the plus `+` button).
   * Fill in the catalog details:
     * **Catalog Title**: `Mangabound`
     * **Catalog URL**: The address shown in Mangabound's Share Panel (e.g. `http://192.168.1.150:8080/opds`).
     * **Username & Password**: Enter credentials only if you set them in Mangabound; otherwise, leave empty.
   * Tap **Save** or **OK**.

4. ### Browse and Download Manga
   * Tap on **Mangabound** from your list of OPDS catalogs.
   * Choose **All Manga** or **Newest Additions**.
   * Tap any volume to view its metadata and tap **Download**.
   * KOReader saves the `.epub` or `.cbz` file directly into your chosen books folder and offers to open it immediately.
</Steps>
