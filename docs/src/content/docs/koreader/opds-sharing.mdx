Connecting USB cables every time you finish converting a manga volume is tedious. Mangabound includes a built-in **OPDS 1.2 (Open Publication Distribution System)** web server, allowing any e-reader on your local Wi-Fi network—such as a Kindle or Kobo running **KOReader**—to browse and download your converted manga wirelessly.

---

## What is OPDS?

OPDS is an open industry standard for cataloging and delivering digital publications via HTTP.
* It presents an XML feed structured into categories (such as *Newest Additions*).
* E-reader apps like KOReader can connect to this feed, display book covers and summaries, and download full `.epub` or `.cbz` volumes with a single tap.

---

## Starting the Server in Mangabound

<Steps>
1. ### Open the Share Panel
   Click on the **Radio Tower** icon in Mangabound's top title bar, or click **Share these books** from the Ready Books screen.

2. ### Select Library & Network Interface
   * **Library to Share**: Choose either your active conversion output folder or an existing manga library on your hard drive.
   * **Network Interface**: Select your Wi-Fi or local Ethernet adapter (e.g. `192.168.1.150`). Mangabound automatically enumerates active local IP addresses.

3. ### Optional Authentication
   If you are on a shared network (such as university or office Wi-Fi), you can set a **Username** and **Password**. On home networks, you can leave both fields blank for frictionless access.

4. ### Click Start Sharing
   Once started, Mangabound displays your unique **Catalog Address**:
   ```
   http://192.168.1.150:8080/opds
   ```
   * Click **Copy** to copy the URL to your clipboard.
   * Keep Mangabound open while your e-reader connects and downloads files.
</Steps>
