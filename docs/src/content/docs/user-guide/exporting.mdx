---
title: Exporting & Ready Books
description: How to save finished books to your storage, open them in your default viewer, or organize your local library.
---

When conversions conclude, Mangabound places the finished files in the **Ready Books** view.

---

## Exporting Options

### 1. Save As… (Single Book)
Click **Save as…** next to any ready book to open your system's native save dialog. Choose the desired folder on your computer, external hard drive, or directly on a connected e-reader mounted over USB.

### 2. Save All to Folder… (Batch Export)
If you converted an entire series consisting of multiple volumes:
1. Click **Save all to folder…** at the top of the ready list.
2. Select your destination folder (e.g. `D:/Manga/Berserk/`).
3. Mangabound safely exports every ready volume. Existing files with matching names are never overwritten automatically.

:::note[Last Save Location Memory]
Mangabound remembers your last used export directory and suggests it during subsequent saves, saving you repetitive folder browsing.
:::

---

## Managing Ready Books

* **Open Book**: Clicking the title or book icon immediately launches the file in your operating system's default viewer (e.g., Calibre, SumatraPDF, or Apple Books).
* **Delete from Ready Books**: If a book was processed by mistake or is no longer needed in the temporary queue, click the trash icon. A confirmation prompt ensures you don't delete it by accident. Removing a book from Ready Books only clears it from Mangabound's internal queue; any copies already saved to disk are left untouched.
* **Send to KOReader**: Click **Share these books** to open the wireless OPDS delivery drawer without needing to save files to disk first!
