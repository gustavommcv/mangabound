Mangabound integrates **mangapress** to deliver pixel-perfect conversions tailored to your exact e-reader model. Instead of stretching or letterboxing images, mangapress resizes, crops, and optimizes manga pages for the physical resolution and e-ink palette of your device.

## Output Formats

* **EPUB (Recommended)**: The standard fixed-layout format for KOReader, Kobo, and modern e-readers. Preserves exact page dimensions, supports table of contents navigation, and loads instantly.
* **CBZ**: An archive containing the processed, cropped, and resized images. Ideal if your reader prefers raw archives or if you are using image viewer apps.
* **PDF**: Fixed document format suitable for large-format tablets and reMarkable devices.

## Image Processing Options

### 1. Margins & Page Numbers Cropping
Scanlations and digital rips often contain wide, uneven white margins and distracting digital page numbering.
* **Margins & Page Numbers (Default)**: Detects outer gutters and eliminates blank borders and marginal numbers, letting manga panels occupy the maximum screen area.
* **Margins Only**: Removes white padding while preserving peripheral annotations.
* **Disabled**: Keeps raw original borders intact.

### 2. Double-Page Spreads (Splitter)
Manga frequently features panoramic two-page spreads. You can choose how they should be delivered:
* **Split (Default)**: Automatically slices the spread down the center into two separate vertical pages. In accordance with Japanese manga conventions (**Manga Style RTL**), the **right page is shown first**, followed by the **left page**.
* **Rotate**: Rotates the double spread by 90 degrees so you can view it horizontally in landscape mode.
* **Both**: Generates both the rotated full spread and the split halves.

### 3. Screen Optimization: Erase Rainbow / Moiré
On color e-ink screens (such as E Ink Kaleido or Gallery 3 panels) and high-resolution monochromatic readers, dense halftone screentones often cause distracting **moiré interference patterns** (rainbow waves).
* Toggling **Erase Rainbow** applies a frequency filter to attenuate moiré artifacts while keeping line art crisp and sharp.

### 4. Create One Book for the Series
For long-running series, checking **Create one book for the series** compiles all volumes into a single cohesive `.epub` file. The internal Table of Contents is structured hierarchically, nesting individual chapters beneath their respective volume headers.
