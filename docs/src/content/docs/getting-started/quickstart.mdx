Welcome to the Mangabound Quick Start guide. In this walkthrough, you'll see how to prepare a series, organize its chapters into volumes, convert it for your e-reader, and optionally send it over Wi-Fi to KOReader.

---

## The 5-Step Conversion Workflow

<Steps>
1. ### Add Your Manga
   Launch Mangabound. You will be greeted by the **Queue Screen**.
   
   * **Drag and drop** a folder of manga chapters, individual `.cbz` files, or an entire manga library folder directly into the window.
   * Alternatively, click the large drop area to open your system's native folder picker.

   :::tip[Visual Media Placeholder]
   > 📹 **GIF Demo**: Dragging a manga folder onto Mangabound's drop target and seeing it added to the conversion queue.
   :::

2. ### Check Volume Mappings
   Mangabound immediately executes a background dry-run using `mangabind` to parse chapter names.
   
   * If folder names include volume information (e.g. `Vol.01 Ch.001 - Title`), chapters are **automatically assigned** to their corresponding volumes.
   * If folder names only contain chapter numbers (`Chapter 01`, `Chapter 02`), the **Volume Mapping Editor** will flag them as unassigned:
     * Click **From MangaDex** to search the series title online and fetch official volume chapter counts.
     * Or click **Auto-Assign** to let Mangabound detect volume boundaries from patterns.
     * You can also drag chapters, create new volumes, or split ranges manually.

   :::tip[Visual Media Placeholder]
   > 📸 **Screenshot / GIF**: The Volume Mapping Editor showing chapters grouped into Volume 1 and Volume 2 with MangaDex sync.
   :::

3. ### Choose Your E-Reader Settings
   In the **Settings Panel** on the right side:
   
   * **Process Steps**:
     * *Regroup into CBZ*: Consolidates chapters into clean `.cbz` files per volume.
     * *Convert for e-reader*: Applies the `mangapress` image processing pipeline.
     * *Both*: Does both steps in a single automated pass.
   * **Device Profile**: Select your target hardware (e.g., **Kindle Paperwhite**, **Kindle Oasis**, **Kobo Libra 2**, **reMarkable 2**).
   * **Format**: Choose between **EPUB** (recommended for KOReader and native reading), **CBZ**, or **PDF**.
   * **Manga Reading Direction**: Enabled by default (right-to-left spread splitting).

4. ### Start Processing
   Click the **Start Conversion** button.
   
   * Mangabound will switch to the **Running Screen**.
   * As `mangapress` processes each page, you will see real-time updates:
     * Current stage: *Inspecting*, *Cropping margins*, *Optimizing images*, *Packaging EPUB*.
     * Precise page counter: e.g. *Page 84 of 192*.
   * If needed, conversions can be safely cancelled at any moment.

   :::tip[Visual Media Placeholder]
   > 📹 **GIF Demo**: Live progress bar ticking page-by-page as mangapress converts a volume in parallel.
   :::

5. ### Save or Share to KOReader
   Once finished, your items appear in **Ready Books**:
   
   * **Save to Disk**: Click **Save as…** for an individual book or **Save all to folder…** for a series export.
   * **Send to KOReader (Over Wi-Fi)**: Click **Share these books** to start the local OPDS 1.2 server. In KOReader, navigate to OPDS catalogs and download your new books wirelessly!
</Steps>
