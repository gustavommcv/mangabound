# Mangabound

**Mangabound** is a desktop application that turns manga you already have, as folders of chapters or as CBZ files, into books ready for your e-reader.

## What It Does

1. **Add**: Drag manga folders, libraries, or `.cbz` files onto the queue.
2. **Check Volumes**: If folder names carry volume numbers (`Vol.01 Ch.001`), chapters are pre-grouped. If not, the interactive volume editor fetches official groupings from **MangaDex** or lets you assign chapters by hand with full undo/redo.
3. **Choose Process**: Regroup into CBZ, convert for an e-reader, or both. Pick your target device (**Kindle**, **Kobo**, **reMarkable**) and format (**EPUB**, **CBZ**, **PDF**).
4. **Process**: Items run with real-time page-by-page progress and full cancellation support.
5. **Save or Share**: Save individual books, export full series in batch, or share directly over your local Wi-Fi to **KOReader** via the built-in OPDS catalog.

## The Ecosystem

Mangabound is a graphical front-end that orchestrates two autonomous command-line tools:

* **[mangabind](https://github.com/gustavommcv/mangabind)**: Regroups chapters into one `.cbz` per volume with `ComicInfo.xml` metadata.
* **[mangapress](https://github.com/gustavommcv/mangapress)**: Resizes, contrast-tunes, auto-crops margins, and splits spreads for e-readers in Rust.
